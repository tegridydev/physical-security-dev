Using this reference#

The articles explain protocols, systems and integration work. Use the linked specifications and product documentation for exact requirements. A published standard, an implemented feature and a tested installation are separate things.

Read source selection, verification and testing, coverage limits and authorised use.

Report a correction#

Include the page, the claim, the relevant primary source and the affected version. A reproducible example helps with code issues. Remove credentials, customer details and real access records before sharing anything.

Open an issue or follow the contribution guide.

About the website#

The site serves static HTML. Reading and navigation work without JavaScript. Search runs locally in your browser. There are no accounts, comments, tracking scripts or external fonts.

The source is maintained by tegridydev under the MIT licence. Referenced standards and vendor material remain subject to their own terms.

See the 29 September 2026 source corrections for the claims checked and the limits of those checks.
