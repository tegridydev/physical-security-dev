Perimeter and detection systems

Perimeter systems combine sensors, assessment and response. Keep the source and uncertainty with each detection as it moves through the workflow.

On this page
  • Overview
  • Pages

Overview#

Effective perimeter security layers deterrence, delay, detection, assessment, response, and recovery. No sensor independently proves intent or identity; fuse observations while preserving each source and uncertainty.

Pages#

  • Perimeter security architecture
  • ANPR/LPR systems
  • Radar, fence, and buried detection
  • Gates, barriers, and vehicle access

IEC 62676-4:2025 supplies current video surveillance application guideline scope. IEC 62676-6:2026 covers testing/grading real time intelligent video analysis, including intrusion and complex scenarios under operating stress. Powered gates/barriers are machinery: apply the local product standard and risk process; ISO 12100:2010 provides general machinery risk assessment principles and is expected to be replaced, so check current status.
