# Intercom and emergency-communication systems

Voice/video communication, entry release, emergency calling, and mass notification can share infrastructure but have different authority, availability, intelligibility, privacy, and life-safety requirements.

- [Intercom system architecture](intercom-system-architecture.md)
- [Call routing, media, and door control](call-routing-media-and-door-control.md)

## Source baseline

The 2026 second editions of [IEC 62820-1-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68745) and [IEC 62820-1-2](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68746) cover general and IP building-intercom requirements. [IEC 62820-2:2017](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/32331) addresses advanced security intercom for emergency/danger verification and instruction. [ISO 7240-19:2007](https://www.iso.org/standard/42979.html) remains confirmed current as of 2025 for emergency-purpose sound systems, but local adopted fire/emergency standards govern deployment.
